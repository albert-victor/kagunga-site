How milk leaves

How you buy milk

Litres, butterfat, and a standing collection are agreed from the herd on Magoda Street.

  1. Raw milk, same morning

    Most highland milk still leaves the farm raw, in a can, the morning it is drawn. Processors and hotels that take a regular line care about cooling, cleanliness of the pail, and whether water has been added.

  2. A route, not a single litre

    A one-off can and a daily morning collection are different products. Regular buyers price the same hour, from the same herd. People in town buy what is left that day.

  3. What shows up in the bucket

    Mastitis, ticks, and a gap in feed all thin or spoil the milk. Cool Njombe nights help. They do not replace a clean can or a herd milked at the same hours.
