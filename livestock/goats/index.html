At the gate

How goats leave

Goats here live on browse, crop residues, and a dry night shelter. Worms, orf, and wet-season pneumonia show up in the animal you buy. A flock that has been kept, not just gathered, is the difference a butcher can see.

  1. Meat first, then breeding

    Goat trade in the Southern Highlands is mainly meat. Castrated males and finished young stock move first. Breeding does are a second line, priced on age, udder, and whether they have already kidded.

  2. Condition on highland keep

    Goats here live on browse, crop residues, and a dry night shelter. Worms, orf, and wet-season pneumonia show up in the animal you buy. A flock that has been kept, not just gathered, is the difference a butcher can see.

  3. Price by head or live weight

    Most farm-gate sales are by the head after a walk-round. Larger lots sometimes move on estimated live weight. A few head for a ceremony and a regular collection for a butcher are two different trades.

  4. Movement

    Animals leaving the district need the movement papers the local livestock office issues. That is ordinary Tanzania livestock law. Plan it before the truck arrives.
