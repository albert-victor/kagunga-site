About us

A registered farm company in Njombe Town

We sit on Magoda Street. Avocado is the main land. Livestock is the second work. You deal with one company.

Home / About

Who we are

The legal name is Kagunga Farms Company Limited. We grow avocado on more than 27 hectares and keep cattle, goats, and poultry on the same farm.

Fruit is picked for packers and traders who serve export markets. Cattle, goats, and poultry are for buyers in Njombe and beyond.

Where we work

Njombe Town is in the Southern Highlands of Tanzania. Cool nights, rain in season, soils that already hold trees. Magoda Street is how you find us – phone, WhatsApp, or a visit.
