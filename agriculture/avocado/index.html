Avocado

The orchard

We farm more than 27 hectares of avocado in Njombe. That is the land you walk, and the land we plan a pick from.

Njombe sits in the Southern Highlands. Cool nights and rain in season are why avocado grows here. Come to the rows. We confirm the trees and this season when you visit or write.

From the farm

How fruit leaves

We grow. Packers and traders who serve export markets take the fruit. If you need a named destination, ask us what we can put in writing.

  1. Picked when it is ready

    We pick when the fruit is ready to travel – dry matter and firmness, not the colour of the skin on the tree. Early fruit travels poorly; fruit left too long does not travel. We test before a commercial pick, then move it the same day.

  2. Clean fruit, ready to pack

    Intact skin, kept out of field heat. Clip-harvest, shade on the farm, and same-day movement. That is how we hand fruit to packers and traders.

  3. A farm you can walk

    Come. See the block, agree which rows, and plan the week from the orchard – not from a roadside pile.

  4. The week, confirmed on the trees

    The Southern Highland season is the calendar. We confirm the pick week on this orchard when you ask.
