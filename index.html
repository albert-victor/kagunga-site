Why Kagunga

Why people work with us

Highland ground

Njombe is cool, with rain in season, and soils that already grow avocado, tea, and timber. That is why we farm here.

Trees and animals together

The orchard and the herds are one company. Manure goes back to the land. You deal with one office.

Come and see the orchard

Walk the rows, look at the fruit, and plan a pick with us. Come, we will show you.

A clear way to buy

Fruit goes to packers and traders who serve export markets. Animals leave when we agree. Next is a call, or a day on Magoda Street.

The region

Njombe, Southern Highlands

Njombe sits in Tanzania’s Southern Highlands. It is cooler than the coast. In a normal year the rain is enough for trees. These soils have long grown timber, potatoes, tea, and avocado.
